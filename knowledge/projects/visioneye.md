# VisionEye: AI-Powered Zone-Based Surveillance Analytics

## Overview
A computer-vision project built with Python, Flask, Ultralytics YOLOv10 and OpenCV. Users upload surveillance or traffic videos, define custom polygon zones, and detect and count vehicles and persons inside them.

## Output
It produces annotated output videos with bounding boxes, labels, zone overlays and frame details, plus zone-wise detection summaries.

## Technologies
Python, Flask, YOLOv10, OpenCV, computer vision, deep learning.
