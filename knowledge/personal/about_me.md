# About Ajanta

## Summary
Ajanta Luhana is an AI Software Engineer at Pixis Software. She builds AI-powered software with AI/ML, LLMs, AI agents, RAG and automation, plus the backend services and APIs that turn AI ideas into practical, reliable applications.

## Background
Before Pixis Software, Ajanta worked as an Artificial Intelligence Engineer at Metavystic, and earlier as an AI Consultant at Pixis and a Data Analyst at Voguetech. She is a Bachelor of Engineering student (Batch 19) at Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET), Jamshoro.

## Focus areas
Her focus areas are LLMs, RAG and AI agents. She builds intelligent systems, AI-powered products and data-driven solutions for real-world problems.

## Leadership and community
Ajanta served as a Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador at MUET, organising and leading workshops, hackathons and tech talks on cloud computing, AI and software development using Azure, Power BI and GitHub.

She is a member of the IEEE ComSoc MUET Student Chapter, where she helped organise seminars and discussions on 5G, IoT and satellite communications.

During COVID-19 she did youth community work, leading local volunteers distributing food, medicine and hygiene kits and spreading accurate vaccination and prevention information.
